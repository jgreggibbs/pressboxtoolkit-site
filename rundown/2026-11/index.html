Introducing Press Box MEET

Meet announcing has its own rhythm: events, heats, lanes, flights, rotations, and results read back as the scoring software reports them. Press Box MEET is built for exactly that, with support for swim and dive, track and field, and gymnastics.

MEET never decides a place, a score, or a record. It reads what the officials and meet software report and helps you say it well.

Set up a meet in minutes

  • Import Hy-Tek Meet Manager heat sheets as text or PDF, and review the events, heats, athletes, and teams before anything is saved. Or build the meet by hand, lane by lane, straight from a printed heat sheet.
  • Pronunciations fill in automatically for every athlete, using the same name engine as Pro, and you can type your own over them.

Run the meet one step at a time

  • Each heat appears on screen with its script ready to read: event, heat, lane, school, and name with pronunciation. Relays read their legs in order.
  • Read results as Top 3 or every place, with ties and DQs worded properly, plus team score updates.
  • Mix in announcements and sponsor reads wherever you want them. Tap Done, Next to move on. A reload picks up where you left off, and the screen stays awake.
  • Print the whole session with blanks to write in results.

Built for each sport

  • Diving: MEET reads dive numbers as words ("105B: forward two and a half somersaults, pike, degree of difficulty 2.4") and walks diver by diver, round by round, with standings.
  • Swimming: prelims and finals, plus meet, pool, conference, and school records, with a flag when a result ties or beats one.
  • Track and field: jumps and throws attempt by attempt, high jump and pole vault height by height, and ready-made field updates to read between races.
  • Gymnastics: a routine-by-routine walk, rotations grouped by apparatus, and team scores after each rotation.

Works with your other apps. Sponsor lists from Pro or BC and announcements from BC come into MEET as reads.

Try it now. MEET has three demo meets ready to explore (a college swim and dive dual, a high school track invitational, and a college gymnastics dual) plus a built-in guide. If you run a heat sheet through MEET that doesn't read cleanly, send it to us and we'll make it work.

Steadier on game day

  • Opens with no wifi. Once you've opened a tool on a device, it loads again even with no signal in the press box.
  • One tap, one sound. A quick double tap no longer starts two copies of a song.
  • The music keeps playing when a pop-up question appears. Fades and auto-play carry on behind it.
  • Bigger buttons mid-game. Play, Pause, Fade, and Stop are easier to hit, and in Pro, Stop has its own row so a near miss on Fade doesn't cut the music dead.
  • Smoother mic duck in Pro, with no pop.
  • Ask before deleting. Trash icons now confirm before anything is removed.
  • A warning when your work isn't saving, instead of failing silently.
  • Faster everywhere, with smaller downloads and less work on every tap.

Also new

  • Press Box LITE: copy tasks to other sections, move or copy soundboard buttons and whole soundboards between games without re-uploading audio, team name variables so cloned games read right, and long button titles shown in full. Most of these came straight from LITE users.
  • Beyond high school: Men's and Women's teams at College and Adult levels, so a college or club team reads properly. "Visitor" is now "Away" everywhere, and your old scripts still work.
  • Pro: a game notes space in the live Scoring tab for soccer, field hockey, and hockey, requested by a field hockey user.
  • PA: adjustable text size and drag-by-grip, so scrolling the rundown doesn't grab a card.
  • Roster Manager: one team at a time on a phone, and backup for all your teams at once.

Keep the requests coming. Many of the items in this month's list started as a note from one of you!

Spotify support has ended

As we announced last month, Spotify support ended November 1. Your old Spotify buttons in DJ and LITE are still there with their titles and colors, now showing the Spotify link so you know which song to upload. In Pro, Spotify tasks became ordinary tasks with the link in their notes. Need help moving your audio over? Just email us.

What's happening behind the scenes

On the mic at the CP Football World Cups

In October, I spent eleven incredible days as a volunteer PA announcer for the IFCPF 2026 CP Football World Cups at the Arthur M. Blank U.S. Soccer National Training Center in Fayetteville, Georgia. Fourteen nations came to compete in the men's and women's world championships for footballers with cerebral palsy and related conditions.

I ran many of my 24 matches from Press Box Toolkit, with scripts, rosters, and music all in one place. I built pronunciation guides for every team and confirmed names in person with players, coaches, and families, which turned out to be one of the best parts of the week. It was a blast!

Two panels at Georgia Tech

October brought two chances to be back on campus at Georgia Tech's Scheller College of Business.

  • AI in Business @ GT Industry Panel (October 15): I joined fellow panelists Shruthi Shetty and Madeline Poch for a conversation about how technology is reshaping business, how roles keep evolving, and how students can prepare for what's next. Thanks to organizers Saanvi Kankipati and Michael Kolby.
  • Sports & Entertainment Media (October 23): Photographer and filmmaker Travis Ellison, Atlanta United broadcaster Jason Longshore, and I talked with a packed room of students, faculty, and friends about how sports media is changing. Thanks to Dr. Jacqueline Garner and the Minor in the Business of Sports and Entertainment for hosting, and to Dick Bergmark, whose generosity made the minor possible.

At both events, students asked what advice we'd give them. Mine was simple: get involved, stay engaged, and try new things. That's exactly how Press Box Toolkit got its start.
