The Rundown · September 2026

Soundboards rebuilt, sponsor management, and more

Published September 14, 2026 · Greg Gibbs, founder

School is back in session and the fall sports season is officially underway. First whistles have blown, first rundowns have been announced, and we've been heads-down shipping the features you asked for all summer. Here's what's new, what's changing, and what's coming next at Press Box Toolkit.

We also know things break at the worst possible moment. If you need help building a game, importing rosters, organizing your audio, setting up sponsors, or just talking through how your game day runs, we're happy to help. Drop us an email and we'll find a convenient time. There's no cost and no catch.

New this month

We've released nearly 100 updates since the July newsletter. Most of them started as a note from one of you. Here are the big ones:

Soundboards, rebuilt

Every game gets its own soundboard now, split into sections by phase. Each trigger carries its own volume, ducking, fades, and loop settings, and one trigger can auto-play the next with a crossfade. No more hunting through a single shared audio library. You upload your files right where you use them.

Sponsor management

Set your sponsorship levels and how often each sponsor should be read, and PBT spreads those reads evenly across the game and places them in your rundown for you. A live panel tracks the tally as you go, and every read is logged, so when a sponsor asks what they got, you can hand them an actual report.

Rundown templates

Build your show once and reuse it all season. Templates store school, mascot, and venue as placeholders and fill them in automatically from whatever game you import into.

Live variables and auto-filled scores

Drop a variable like {{home}}, {{visitor}}, or {{venue}} into any task or sponsor read and it fills itself in. Enter the score at the end of each quarter or period and your announcer scripts fill in the blanks themselves. There's also a Next Home Game Promo preset ready to go.

Emergency plans and handoff

Emergency contacts, procedures, and ready-to-read PA announcements all live in the app now. And if you can't make a game, the new Hand Off button emails a complete backup to whoever is covering for you.

Two new sports

Flag Football and Softball, across all six apps.

Plus better name pronunciations from a real pronunciation dictionary, team colors on your game-day screens, at-a-glance readiness indicators so you know what still needs setup, and a long list of iPad fixes.

Let us know what's still missing or broken!

Heads up: Spotify support ends Nov 1

To avoid having our users violate Spotify's terms of service, we're retiring the Spotify integration, and Spotify triggers will stop working on November 1, 2026. You can't add new ones as of now, and existing ones will be removed at the sunset date.

The good news is that the soundboard triggers have been greatly improved, with per-trigger volume, fades, looping, and crossfades. If you have Spotify buttons in your setup, reach out and we'll help you move that audio over before your next home game.
